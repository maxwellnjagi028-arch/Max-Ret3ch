## 🔒 Human-In-The-Loop Governance Architecture

To ensure safety and commercial accountability, sensitive actions strictly require **Human Executive Approval**:
1. **Commercial Proposals & Quotations**: All quotations generated by Ted enter a `pending_approval` state. Only when an Admin or Staff member signs off with approval notes can the proposal be dispatched to the client or digitally signed.
2. **On-Site Technician Dispatches**: High-severity repair tickets require staff confirmation before physical dispatch.
3. **ESG Carbon Certificates**: Green e-waste certificates are serialized and require authorized staff verification.
